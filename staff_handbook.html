Basic Knowledge

Most common mistakes

  • You CANNOT Moderate or Use Commands / Use Staff Equipment, Under 5 Players.

  • Spelling, Punctuation, and Grammar (SPaG).

  • Do not park in Banned Areas (Middle of Roads, Areas that Block Traffic)

  • The 4 Letter Command Rule:
    You MUST use 4+ letters when doing ANY MODERATING COMMANDS. For example, do “:bring skimy”, don’t do “:bring ski.”

  • Not adhering to company policies and procedures.

Warning & Strike System

  • 3 warnings = 1 strike
  • 3 Strikes = Zero Tolerance Policy (ZTP) for 1 week If you get warned/striked while on ZTP you will get terminated
  • 2 Terminations = Staff Blacklist
  • Breach of ToS = Termination / Blacklist
  • Under Investigation = Suspension

Quota

  • Moderation Team - 5 hours a month of moderating
  • Administration Team - 5 hours a month of moderating
  • Internal Affairs Team - 3 hours a month moderating including High Rank tickets
  • Management Team - 4 hours a month moderating + High Rank ticket
  • Directive Team - 2 Hours General server management + Tickets + Active in-game and discord

Leave of Absence

  • To apply for an LOA, please head to melonly and request an LOA
  • LOA can not exceed 14+ days. (Unless a good reason provided)
  • After requesting an LOA, you have to wait 1+ weeks before you can get another one
